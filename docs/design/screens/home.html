Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order?
We recommend placing your order at least 2 weeks in advance for standard designs, and 4-6 weeks for complex or large tier cakes.
Do you cater to dietary requirements?
Yes, we offer gluten-free, vegan, and nut-free options. Please specify your requirements during the design process. Note that our kitchen handles nuts and gluten.
Can I schedule a tasting?
Tasting boxes are available for order online and can be collected from our atelier or delivered within Paris. We offer consultation appointments for wedding cakes over $500.
How do I pay?
A 50% deposit is required to secure your order, with the balance due 7 days prior to collection or delivery. We accept all major credit cards.
What if I need to cancel?
Cancellations made more than 14 days before the event will receive a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations within 14 days are non-refundable.
